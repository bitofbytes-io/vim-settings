# Third-party colorscheme

`colors/monokai.vim` is a bundled conversion of the original TextMate Monokai
theme. Its existing header is preserved.

- **Original Monokai:** Wimer Hazenberg, created in 2006. Source/history:
  <https://monokai.pro/history>.
- **Converter:** Coloration v0.3.2 by Marcin Kulik (formerly `sickill`, now
  `ku1ik`): <https://github.com/ku1ik/coloration/tree/v0.3.2>.
  Coloration is MIT licensed; its current notice is copyright 2009–2012
  Marcin Kulik: <https://github.com/ku1ik/coloration/blob/master/LICENSE.txt>.
